03 — Approach

Where Copilot rollouts stall, and what I do about it.

Licences without use cases

Everyone gets access, nobody knows what to try first, and usage drops after a fortnight. I start from a ranked list of use cases tied to real work.

Data that isn't ready

Copilot surfaces whatever a user can reach. Oversharing that was invisible for years becomes visible on day one, so permissions come before rollout.

Adoption as a training day

People slip back unless examples come from their own work and someone keeps measuring. Adoption is a programme with a baseline.
